THE BMW iX1 LONG WHEELBASE — ELECTRIC PERFORMANCE.

The BMW iX1 Long Wheelbase delivers instant torque from its electric motor,
providing responsive acceleration and a smooth, refined driving experience.
The efficient eDrive technology ensures impressive range for your daily
commute and weekend adventures alike.

ADAPTIVE SUSPENSION

The advanced suspension system ensures exceptional ride comfort and
dynamic handling. It continuously adapts to road conditions, providing
the perfect balance between sportiness and comfort for an outstanding
driving experience.

SUSTAINABILITY IN THE BMW iX1 LONG WHEELBASE.

BMW is committed to sustainability throughout the entire vehicle lifecycle.
From renewable energy in production to recycled materials in the interior,
every aspect minimises environmental impact while maximizing driving
pleasure.

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY.

Every line and surface has been meticulously crafted to create a vehicle
that is both visually stunning and functionally superior. The design language
speaks of innovation while remaining true to BMW's heritage of creating
the ultimate driving machine.

CUTTING-EDGE TECHNOLOGY.

State-of-the-art technology is seamlessly integrated into every aspect.
From the BMW iDrive system to advanced driver assistance features,
technology enhances every moment of the driving experience while
maintaining an intuitive interface.

YOUR PERSONAL LIVING SPACE.

The interior creates a welcoming atmosphere that makes every journey
a pleasure. Premium materials, ambient lighting, and thoughtful design
details combine to create a space that feels like a natural extension
of your personal style.
