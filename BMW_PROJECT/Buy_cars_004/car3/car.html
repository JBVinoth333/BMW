EXPERIENCE THE DESIGN OF THE FULLY ELECTRIC BMW i4 IN THE
VIDEOS.

EXTERIOR DESIGN HIGHLIGHTS.

Striking

The front of the all-electric BMW iX has been
reinterpreted in a futuristic style. The
vertically aligned BMW kidney grille
particularly highlights the progressive
appearance of the SAV.

Aerodynamic

The generously proportioned rectangular
contours around the wheel arches
consciously focus attention on the wheels.
The flush door handles, the frameless doors
and the window outline, known as the
"Streamflow", that tapers towards the rear
are all harmoniously integrated into the
appearance.

Sleek

Large surfaces and a seamlessly integrated
tailgate produce a modern presence. The
large diffuser elements optimise
aerodynamics and, together with the flat
underbody, improve range.

INTERIOR DESIGN HIGHLIGHTS.

Minimalist interior design.

The interior design is modern and pared
down to the essentials – a focus which can
be noted in the displays and controls. As the
central control element, the glassy iDrive
Controller on the centre console is framed by
an elegant control panel.

Panorama glass roof.

The full-length panoramic glass roof creates
a generous sense of space and a relaxed
lounge atmosphere within the cabin, offering
passengers even more headroom with a
modern feel.

Lounge seating in the rear.

The spacious rear offers a comfortable
lounge atmosphere for passengers with
additional options including heating and
premium audio integration.

THE FULLY ELECTRIC BMW i4 — ELECTRIC GRAN COUPÉ PERFORMANCE.

The BMW i4 delivers exhilarating electric performance with instant torque
and a smooth, refined driving experience that redefines the Gran Coupé
segment with zero local emissions.

ADAPTIVE SUSPENSION

The adaptive suspension ensures extremely comfortable driving
with great driving dynamics, keeping the vehicle evenly elevated
regardless of the driving mode.

SUSTAINABILITY IN THE FULLY ELECTRIC BMW i4.

BMW is committed to sustainability throughout the entire vehicle lifecycle.
From renewable energy in production to recycled materials in the interior,
every aspect minimises environmental impact.

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY OF THE BMW i4.

Every line and surface has been meticulously crafted to create a vehicle
that is both visually stunning and functionally superior, speaking of
innovation while remaining true to BMW's heritage.

CUTTING-EDGE TECHNOLOGY.

State-of-the-art technology is seamlessly integrated into every aspect.
From the BMW iDrive system to advanced driver assistance features,
technology enhances every moment of the driving experience.

YOUR PERSONAL LIVING SPACE.

The interior creates a welcoming atmosphere that makes every journey
a pleasure. Premium materials, ambient lighting, and thoughtful design
details combine to create a space that feels uniquely yours.
